    Welcome

    Introductions, and what stays private. Confidentiality is explained before anything else.

    05:00

    Listening

    "What brings you here today?" Then mostly quiet, and attention.

    15:00

    Open questions

    "How did that feel?" rather than "Was that hard?" Questions that leave room.

    25:00

    Saying back what she heard, so the person can hear it too.
