What Aditi said, and what changes

"Too colourful."→A black-and-white base. Colour appears only where it means something, and each direction has a reason for its one colour.
"Blocky. Childish. Pens and clips lying around."→No desk props. Editorial layouts, real typography, her photographs as the main images.
"The background beside the book was bad."→The space around the content is designed: studio light, paper, film, or ink. Never a cluttered desk.
"Transitions too slow."→Everything answers within 250–450 ms. Scrolling moves the story directly instead of playing an animation you have to wait for.
"The crumpled paper meant nothing."→Gone. Every motion now stands for an idea from her field: perception, attention, reflection, conversation.
"Loved the book and the illustrations."→Kept in every direction: the headset, the tally, the stamp, the Braille, the Gibbs cycle.
"Some horizontal scroll."→All four directions move sideways as you scroll.

Plates

Hermann Rorschach's 1921 cards were mostly black ink, then red, then soft pastels, all folded into mirror symmetry. That gives the brief's "black and white with one colour" a reason that comes from psychology itself. Each part of her story is a plate. Scroll sideways and the ink reshapes. Move your cursor over it and you add mirrored ink.

I / VImove over the ink

Scroll by scroll

  1. Plate I: her name beside a black blot, and one question: what do you see?
  2. Each experience is a plate. Between plates, the ink draws back and opens into a new shape.
  3. The plates for helpline and hospital add red, as the real cards II and III do. Care and people turn pastel, like cards VIII–X.
  4. Clicking a plate opens the journal entry.

Signature moment

Your cursor adds ink, mirrored, so every visitor's blot is slightly different. Nobody reads the same plate the same way.

Why it means something

Projective tests are about perception and interpretation, which is a counsellor's core skill. It's used as art, never as a test: the site makes no claims about the visitor.

Effort · risk

Medium · low

One shader drives all of it, so it runs smoothly on phones too. The risk: if the ink is too large, the site feels moody. Keep it airy, with lots of paper.

Scroll by scroll

  1. One long roll of film slides past, one frame per moment of her story.
  2. Captions sit on the paper below, like a photographer's notes.
  3. Grease-pencil circles land on the keepers as they pass.

Signature moment

The loupe: a round magnifier that follows the cursor and shows the frame in colour. Black and white everywhere, colour only where you pay attention.

Why it means something

Attention changes what you see, and that's the heart of counselling. The red marks are reflection: choosing what mattered.

Effort · risk

Low · depends on photos

Simple to build, and it looks expensive. It only works if Aditi sends 8–12 good photos.

  3. The type behind slides sideways, so the whole stage travels left.

Signature moment

The book itself, now on a quiet stage. One deliberate object, a clipped photo, instead of a desk full of props.

Why it means something

A reflective journal is a real tool in counsellor training, and every entry follows Gibbs' cycle. Red appears once, on the hospital's "Excellent" stamp.

Effort · risk

Low · low

Builds directly on round one. The risk: it's the most expected of the four.
